# Keneflex wrist/thumb launch product matrix

Status date: August 25, 2026  
Prototype release: 0.8.0

## Launch rule

Keneflex sells product-selection confidence. Package roles remain stable—support, recovery, comfort, and a product guide—but the products filling those roles must follow the consumer's story. The prototype is not a rehabilitation program and does not prescribe exercises, stretches, or ergonomic programs.

Prices in the prototype are product totals used to test comprehension and package choice. They must be revalidated before commerce is enabled.

## Explicitly deferred

- TENS units
- Supplements and oral medication
- Specialized night products
- Exercise, stretch, rehabilitation, and ergonomic programs
- Body regions beyond the hand/wrist/thumb pathway

## Package logic for retest

- **Essential:** selected support + personalized product guide.
- **Recommended:** Essential + story-matched recovery product. This is the default recommendation unless the consumer clearly wants only a support or explicitly wants a broader package.
- **Complete:** Recommended + story- and format-matched comfort product. It is unavailable when broken skin or a stated topical limitation makes it inappropriate.

Highlighting the middle option is acceptable only when it reflects the actual recommendation. Visual emphasis must update with the recommendation; it cannot be a permanent sales nudge.
