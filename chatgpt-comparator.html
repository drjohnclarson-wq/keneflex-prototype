Live ChatGPT comparison

Do not show a prewritten finished answer. Use a fresh ChatGPT conversation so the participant experiences the prompting and clarification work themselves.

Standardize the setup

  • Use the same moderator-controlled ChatGPT account/configuration for all P1–P6 when practical.
  • Start a fresh chat with no prior Keneflex context.
  • The participant—not the moderator—drives the conversation.
  • Use the same private scenario facts as the Keneflex test when ChatGPT asks for details.

Participant's first message

My hand has been bothering me.

Instruction to participant

“Use ChatGPT the way you normally would if this were your problem. Answer its questions using the scenario facts I give you. You can ask follow-up questions if you normally would. Tell me when you feel you have enough to decide what to do—or when you feel you would go somewhere else.”

Record

  • Participant-message count.
  • Clarifying questions ChatGPT asks.
  • Information the participant has to introduce without being asked.
  • Whether the result includes a specific product, sizing, complete solution, self-care, and decision-ready next step.
  • Whether the participant still wants outside research.
Interpretation: Keneflex always comes first in this six-person formative study so its unprimed response can be measured. That creates an order effect. Treat the ChatGPT comparison as directional, not controlled proof of superiority.
