Self-care plan

  • Temporarily reduce the amount or intensity of gripping/twisting that clearly worsens symptoms.
  • Keep comfortable wrist/thumb motion rather than aggressively stretching into pain.
  • Use the support during activities where it helps, but stop or reassess if it causes numbness, skin irritation or worse function.
  • If you develop new trauma, persistent numbness, marked weakness, deformity, significant swelling, or worsening function, get evaluated rather than continuing to self-treat.

What I would not automatically add

I would not automatically add a second brace, oral pain medication, supplements or sleep products without more information. If you already owned an adequate cold pack or topical, I would use that rather than duplicate it.

Bottom line: For this scenario I’d start with the Neo G Airflow Wrist & Thumb Support in Medium, use a cold/recovery option after aggravating activity if helpful, keep the topical optional, and pair those with load modification and comfortable motion.
