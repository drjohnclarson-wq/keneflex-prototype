## Primary-source verification

Polar Products' current product page identifies SKU SPW8 as the **Soft Ice Wrist Wrap**, designed to hold one 4.5 x 7 inch Soft Ice pack comfortably on either wrist. The listed product includes an insulated plush wrap, attached elastic strap, and one reusable Soft Ice pack. Polar describes the pack as usable for cold or hot therapy and remaining flexible after freezing.

Polar's current wrist/hand category also lists the Soft Ice Wrist Wrap within its wrist/hand pain-relief lineup and describes its Soft Ice compression wraps as providing targeted cold/hot therapy with adjustable compression.
