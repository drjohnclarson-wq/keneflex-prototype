What I’d have you do, not just buy

Modify the aggravating load

Reduce the amount or intensity of gripping/twisting that clearly flares it. You don’t need to stop every activity automatically.

Keep comfortable motion

Move the wrist and thumb gently through comfortable ranges rather than aggressively stretching into pain.

Use recovery after activity

Cold can be reasonable after pickleball or other aggravating use if it feels helpful. Protect the skin and follow product directions.

Watch the trend

Improving function and less soreness are reassuring. New numbness, marked weakness, deformity, significant swelling, or worsening function changes the plan.
