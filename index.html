Your self-care + return-to-activity plan

The plan is part of the solution—not fine print after the products.

Reduce the aggravating dose

Temporarily reduce the amount/intensity of gripping and twisting that predictably increases symptoms rather than treating “rest” as all-or-nothing.

Keep it gently moving

Use comfortable wrist/thumb motion through a tolerable range. Do not force painful stretching just to feel like you're doing more.

After activity: cool + recover

If cold feels helpful, use the recovery component according to its directions. Do not apply topical menthol under a heating device or tightly occlusive wrap.

Know when to reassess

Stop self-management and seek appropriate evaluation for significant worsening, new persistent numbness/weakness, deformity, or other concerning changes. Persistent symptoms also deserve reassessment.

How it works

You bring the problem. Keneflex carries the decision work.

1
Tell us what's bothering you.

Use your own words. You do not need to know the diagnosis, anatomy, or product category.

2
We figure out what matters.

Keneflex narrows location, timing, safety, goals, fit, what you already own, preferences and constraints—without asking you to repeat facts you already supplied.

3
We do the product homework.

We compare only options that clear the relevant requirements, then explain why one won and why representative alternatives lost.

4
We build the solution.

That can include a primary product, recovery/comfort components and self-care—or a $0 KEEP answer when what you already own is adequate.

5
You can change the constraints.

Already own part of it? Use a home remedy? Don't value an item? Need to spend less? Tell Keneflex and we rebuild without handing the shopping decision back to you.

Our approach

The recommendation should be earned.

✓
Specific to you

If a relevant fact about you changes, the recommendation should be capable of changing too.

✓
Need before product

Safety and the role the solution must perform come before brand, margin, popularity or inventory.

✓
Only what belongs

More products are not automatically better. An adequate product you already own can beat a new sale.

✓
Evidence before popularity

The intended engine uses product construction, fit, instructions, evidence, failure patterns and eventually Keneflex outcome data—not star ratings alone.

✓
Recommendation before economics

Keneflex may earn money on some purchases, but economics should not make a product eligible or change which solution wins.

Our job is to finish the decision—not give you another list to research.
