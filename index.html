Our approach

The recommendation has to earn the sale.

Specific to you

The answer should change when facts that matter about you change.

Need before product

Keneflex starts with the problem. It can recommend BUY, KEEP, use what you already have, or stop the shopping path.

Evidence before popularity

Star ratings and marketing claims are not enough. We care about who a product fits, who it fails, and why.

Minimum necessary solution

More restriction and more products are not automatically better.

Your constraints matter

Budget, preferences, home remedies and existing products should change the solution without making you re-shop everything.

Commercial firewall

Keneflex may earn money when you buy. Economics cannot decide suitability or make a product win.

Our job is to finish the decision—not give you another list to research.
