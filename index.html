4 · Self-care is part of the solution

Your recovery plan

Products are only one part of what you do next. Because Keneflex is not diagnosing a specific tissue injury here, the plan stays conservative and avoids aggressive stretching.

01
Modify the load—not your whole lifeTemporarily reduce gripping, twisting, paddle volume, or other motion that clearly increases symptoms. Keep using the hand for comfortable everyday movement rather than immobilizing it all day.
02
Gentle movement, not forced stretchingOnce or twice a day, move the wrist and thumb gently through comfortable ranges for 5–10 easy repetitions. Stop if movement produces sharp pain, numbness, tingling, or clear worsening.
03
After activity: cool + recoverUse the Polar wrap as a cold therapy tool according to its directions if it feels helpful. Because Biofreeze warns against use with heating devices, heat is not part of this combined prototype plan.
04
Use symptoms to guide the returnKeep the support comfortable and reassess the plan if function worsens, symptoms keep recurring, or new numbness/weakness, significant swelling, or trauma appears.
Why this matters: Keneflex is not just telling you what to buy. It is giving the products a role inside a complete, usable plan.
