# Keneflex Hand/Wrist/Thumb Candidate Matrix v0.2

## Purpose
Convert treatment-role requirements into evidence-backed product candidates. Commercial economics are excluded from eligibility scoring. UNKNOWN never equals PASS.

## Gate
A candidate is eligible only when every hard requirement for its assigned role is supported by product-specific evidence or later Keneflex bench/outcome verification. Marketing indication alone is not functional proof.

## 1. Neutral-wrist night support
### ACE Night Wrist Sleep Support (3M)
- Evidence: manufacturer states palmar splint + cushioning beads promote a neutral hand position during sleep; wrist range 5.25–9 in; ambidextrous.
- Current status: PRIMARY-EVIDENCE PASS for neutral-night positioning and sleep use.
- Remaining bench gates: migration overnight, heat/comfort, pressure points, neutral retention across fit range, don/doff.
- Source: https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/b5005595001/

## 2. Wrist + thumb / activity or crossover support
### Ottobock Thumb Spica 28P211
- Evidence: manufacturer explicitly describes immobilization of wrist and thumb in a neutral position; malleable thumb, palmar and dorsal stays; five wrist-size bands.
- Current status: PRIMARY-EVIDENCE PASS for combined wrist+thumb stabilization; unusually strong candidate for crossover bench testing.
- Remaining gates: activity dexterity/grip interference, migration, comfort, exact thumb control, whether neutral wrist is retained under real use.
- Source: https://shop.ottobock.us/us01/en/Orthotics/Bracing-%26-Supports/Elbow-Wrist-Hand/Wrist-%26-Hand/Thumb-Spica/p/28P211

### Breg Apollo Universal Wrist Brace with Thumb Spica
- Evidence: malleable palmar stay + repositionable dorsal stay; designed for wrist/thumb support while allowing maximum hand function; universal left/right sizing.
- Current status: PRIMARY-EVIDENCE PASS for combined support architecture; neutral-position requirement remains UNKNOWN because manufacturer does not explicitly verify neutral retention.
- Remaining gates: neutral position, thumb control, dexterity, universal-fit failure rate.
- Source: https://www.breg.com/products/elbow-wrist-bracing/wrist/apollo-universal-wrist-brace-thumb-spica/

### Aircast A2 Wrist Brace with Thumb Spica (Enovis)
- Evidence: malleable volar and thumb stay, three wrist-circumference sizes, intended for thumb injuries/De Quervain-type symptoms.
- Current status: PASS for adjustable wrist+thumb stabilization architecture; neutral retention UNKNOWN.
- Remaining gates: same as above plus fit at overlapping size boundaries.
- Source: https://enovis.com/products/aircast/a2-wrist-brace-thumb-spica

## 3. CMC-focused support
### 3 Point Products CMCcare Thumb Brace
- Evidence: manufacturer states CMC stabilization, embedded stay around first metacarpal, alignment support, functional pinch/grip, low-profile waterproof construction and under-glove use.
- Current status: PRIMARY-EVIDENCE PASS for CMC-specific functional role; FAIL for wrist stabilization role by design/scope.
- Remaining gates: pinch strength/function, pressure at web space, migration, sizing reproducibility, prolonged-use comfort.
- Source: https://www.3pointproducts.com/products/scar-care-products/cmccarethumb-brace

## 4. Recovery / cold-heat wrap
### ActiveWrap Wrist Ice & Heat Wrap
- Evidence: product-specific wrist wrap with two reusable gel packs, cold/heat capability, adjustable wrap/compression, flexible-when-frozen claim.
- Current status: substantially better-evidenced candidate for the prototype recovery role than the currently unverified Polar component. ELIGIBLE FOR BENCH TEST, not yet a Keneflex winner.
- Remaining gates: actual wrist/hand coverage, cold duration, skin temperature/safety, pack migration, usable compression without excessive pressure, compatibility with support schedule, cleaning and consumer burden.
- Source: https://activewrap.com/products/wrist-ice-heat-wrap

### Polar Products current wrist/hand recovery component
- Current status: HOLD / NOT ELIGIBLE until exact therapeutic SKU and product-specific evidence are verified. Supplier relationship does not override evidence gate.

## 5. Topical comfort
Biofreeze remains a comfort-role candidate only, not a core treatment substitute. Existing interaction rule remains: label cautions against tight bandaging and heating-device use, so timing/brace interaction must be reconciled in the plan.

## Bench-test priority
1. Ottobock 28P211 vs Breg Apollo vs Aircast A2 for crossover/activity role.
2. ACE Night Wrist Sleep Support for neutral-night role.
3. CMCcare for CMC-specific role.
4. ActiveWrap wrist wrap as replacement candidate for the unverified Polar recovery role.

## Decision consequence
Do not expand the prototype product universe merely because these candidates exist. The immediate objective is to verify whether the current plan's assigned jobs can be performed by defensible products. A product becomes a recommendation winner only after hard functional requirements, fit, usability and safety gates pass; later Keneflex outcome/return data can then supersede manufacturer evidence.
