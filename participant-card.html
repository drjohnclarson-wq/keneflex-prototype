Participant scenario

Use this as if it were happening to you.

This is a prototype, and this is not a test of your medical knowledge. You do not need to know the name of the problem or give the system perfect terminology.

At the beginning, all you know is: “My hand has been bothering me.”

Start Keneflex with only that sentence. If the experience asks for more information about the scenario, the moderator will give you that fact at that time.

Please think naturally. Do not try to guess what Keneflex wants you to say. Tell the moderator what you are thinking as you go. There are no right reactions to the experience.
