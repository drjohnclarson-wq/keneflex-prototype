KENEFLEX SIX-PERSON FORMATIVE PILOT — 0.4.3 FINAL PRE-P1 FLOW

PURPOSE
Test whether a credible representation of Keneflex creates Decision Closure, feels Specific to You, removes meaningful decision work, remains trustworthy when the solution is adjusted, and provides value beyond a strong ChatGPT response.

PARTICIPANTS
P1–P6. Adults with little/no prior Keneflex exposure. One-on-one, not a focus group.

PRIMARY SCENARIO
Use the same participant scenario card for all six. For consistency, the participant begins the prototype by entering only: “My hand has been bothering me.” They answer later questions from the scenario facts only when asked.

MODERATOR RULES
- Do not pitch, rescue, explain, or defend Keneflex during the primary experience.
- Encourage think-aloud behavior, but do not tell participants what they should notice.
- If asked “What should I pick?” respond: “What would you do if I weren’t here?”
- If asked “What does this mean?” respond: “Tell me what you think it means first.”
- If asked “Is this trustworthy?” respond: “What would you need to know to decide that?”
- Record verbatim phrases whenever possible.
- Do not ask participants to open How It Works or Our Approach before the clean Keneflex-only debrief is locked.

PRIMARY EXPERIENCE
1. Participant opens the Keneflex prototype.
2. Participant uses the standardized scenario card.
3. Observe whether vague “hand” language is narrowed naturally.
4. Observe whether the subject sees enough reasoning for the recommendation to feel earned.
5. Participant reviews the full solution.
6. Ask the participant to use “Adjust my solution.” Rotate the secondary constraint:
   - P1/P2: “Imagine you already have a reusable cold pack that works well.”
   - P3/P4: “Imagine you do not want to spend more than $30.”
   - P5/P6: “Imagine you do not like topical pain-relief products.”
7. Observe whether Keneflex re-solves the problem or makes the participant feel they are shopping again.
8. Participant reaches the research stopping point. Do not let them independently research products yet.

DEBRIEF ORDER — DO NOT CHANGE
A. Keneflex-only debrief. Lock it.
B. Moderator observation coding.
C. Show the strong ChatGPT comparator; ask comparison questions.
D. Ask participant to review How It Works + Our Approach; conduct promise-vs-experience audit.
E. Read the Founder-Intent reveal neutrally; collect gap/improvement feedback.
F. Final ratings and close.

CORE SIGNALS
- Decision Closure: intended next action is to act without more product-selection research.
- Specific to You: participant independently says their facts materially changed the solution.
- Decision Work Removed: participant spontaneously identifies research/comparison/sizing/assembly/constraint-handling work Keneflex did.
- Vague-to-structured success: “my hand has been bothering me” is narrowed without requiring clinical vocabulary.
- Solution ownership after tuning: budget/existing-product/preference changes cause Keneflex to re-solve rather than return shopping work to the user.
- Complete solution: participant perceives products + self-care + boundaries as one coherent answer.
- Diligence without re-shopping: rejected alternatives build confidence without triggering comparison shopping.
- Commercial trust: checkout feels like the natural conclusion of judgment, not the reason for the judgment.
- ChatGPT differentiation: participant independently articulates substantive value beyond a strong general-AI answer.
- Promise-to-experience gap: what Keneflex claims in How It Works/Our Approach that subjects did or did not actually experience.

DIRECTIONAL GATE AFTER P6
This is formative, not statistical proof. Strong directional evidence would be:
- 5/6 genuine or near-genuine Decision Closure.
- 5/6 Specific to You.
- Most participants describe meaningful Decision Work Removed.
- Most say solution tuning preserved Keneflex ownership rather than making them shop.
- No more than 1/6 feels compelled to resume product-selection research before acting.
- Most understand why the winner won.
- Rejected alternatives generally do not reopen shopping.
- Participants can articulate a meaningful Keneflex advantage over the strong ChatGPT comparator.
- Founder-intent reveal produces refinements, not a reaction of “that is a completely different product from what I experienced.”

BETWEEN-SESSION RULE
Do not change the prototype for ordinary feedback between P1 and P6. Change only for a safety issue, a technical defect that prevents completion, or materially broken branching. Record any unavoidable version change.

AFTER EACH SESSION
Complete the debrief immediately. Preserve raw recording/transcript if consented. Save by participant ID only.

AFTER P6
Stop testing and synthesize all six together. Do not optimize from a single participant’s preference.
